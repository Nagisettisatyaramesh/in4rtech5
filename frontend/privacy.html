Privacy policy

Last updated: September 2026

This starter policy explains the basic handling of information submitted through this demonstration website. Before publishing, replace it with a policy reviewed for your organization, location and actual practices.

Information you provide

When you use the contact form, the site collects your name, email address and any optional details you provide, including phone, company, service interest and message. The backend stores these details in the Cloud Firestore database for project in4rtech-f4510 so the team can respond.

How information is used

Contact information is used to review and respond to your inquiry, coordinate requested services and maintain business records. It is not sold through this starter application.

Retention and security

Submissions remain in Cloud Firestore until an administrator deletes them or your organization applies its retention process. The backend uses Firebase Admin credentials and restricts the admin endpoints to authenticated sessions. No website can guarantee absolute security.

Cookies and third parties

The admin area uses a session cookie when an administrator signs in. The frontend loads fonts from Google Fonts; you can remove that connection in the stylesheet and use system fonts if you do not want a third-party font request.

Your choices

To request access, correction or deletion of information you submitted, contact your organization through its published business contact channel. Add the correct contact details and any jurisdiction-specific rights before production use.
